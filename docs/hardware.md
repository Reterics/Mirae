# Hardware

## Shopping list

Buy these separately:

- Raspberry Pi 5
- Cooling for the Raspberry Pi 5: any cooler or passive case that fits within 91 × 65 × 34 mm (the space the enclosure reserves), for example the JOY-IT RB-AlucaseP5-09. The Pi stands on four 15 mm spacers (in the list below); fix it to them with 4 × M2.5 screws of the length your case needs.
- Optional: Raspberry Pi AI HAT+ 2 (40 TOPS) with its heatsink. It sits under the Pi on the tray standoffs, not on the GPIO header, so it also needs a PCIe FFC long enough for a 180° loop of about 43 mm (same contact orientation as the cable supplied with the HAT; not chosen yet) and two jumper wires for 5 V and GND.
- Adafruit VL53L1X Time-of-Flight sensor, product 3967 (the sensor mount fits its 4-hole pattern)

## Main computer

The Raspberry Pi 5 handles camera processing, speech recognition, text-to-speech, behavior logic, sensor processing, motor control, communication with AI services and the local web/API interface.

## Drive system

The first version uses a differential-drive layout:

```text
            FRONT
       ┌───────────────┐
       │    CAMERA     │
       └───────────────┘
             ○
         BALL CASTER

        O             O
     LEFT           RIGHT
     MOTOR          MOTOR
            REAR
```

The wheels sit 30 mm behind the centre of the 210 mm body and the caster far forward: a 97 mm wheelbase and a 139 mm track. The caster carries about 37% of the weight (see the [mass budget](parts.md#mass-budget)).

Encoders enable speed control, distance estimation, PID control, straighter driving and basic odometry.

## Sensors

- **Camera Module 3 NoIR** — object and person detection, person tracking, visual navigation, future VLM experiments.
- **VL53L1X ToF** — short-range obstacle detection and collision avoidance. Future versions may use multiple ToF sensors or LiDAR.

## Audio

- **INMP441** — voice recording, wake-word detection, speech recognition, environmental audio.
- **MAX98357 + 4 Ω / 3 W speaker** — text-to-speech, sound effects, status feedback.

## Enclosure

A small, minimal cube-like body, roughly 120–150 mm wide, designed as modular parts rather than a single shell so individual components can change without redesigning the whole robot:

```text
┌─────────────────────┐
│      TOP COVER      │
├─────────────────────┤
│     PI 5 MODULE     │
├─────────┬───────────┤
│ MOTOR   │   MOTOR   │
│ MOUNT   │   MOUNT   │
└─────────┴───────────┘
```

It provides mounting points for the Pi 5 and its cooling case, camera, microphone, speaker, motor driver, both motors, wheels, ball caster, VL53L1X, wiring and the 2S2P battery/power system.

See [`cad/README.md`](../cad/README.md) for printable STLs, print settings and enclosure assembly instructions.
